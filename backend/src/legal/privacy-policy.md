# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: {{ date }}**

## 1. Introduction

SANA CDC ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, password, profile photo
- **Health Information**: Health questionnaire responses, SANA Index scores, daily check-ins, journal entries
- **Practitioner Information**: Professional credentials, qualifications, practice details, availability
- **Payment Information**: Processed securely through Stripe (we do not store full card details)
- **Communication Data**: Messages between clients and practitioners, session notes

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited
- **Analytics Data**: How you interact with our platform, features used
- **Cookies**: See our Cookie Policy for details

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain our services
- Process bookings and payments
- Calculate and display your SANA Index score
- Facilitate communication between clients and practitioners
- Send appointment reminders and notifications
- Improve our platform and user experience
- Comply with legal obligations
- Prevent fraud and ensure security

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:
- **Contract Performance**: To provide services you've requested
- **Consent**: For health data processing and marketing communications
- **Legitimate Interest**: To improve our services and prevent fraud
- **Legal Obligation**: To comply with applicable laws

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:
- **Practitioners**: When you book an appointment, your relevant health information is shared
- **Service Providers**: Stripe (payments), AWS/Cloudflare (hosting), email providers
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties.

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security audits
- Secure payment processing via Stripe

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restriction**: Limit how we process your data
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Object**: Object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@sana-cdc.com

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as:
- Your account is active
- Required to provide services
- Required by law (e.g., financial records for 7 years)

After account deletion, we may retain anonymized data for analytics.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred and processed outside the UK/EU. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions).

## 10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email or platform notification.
